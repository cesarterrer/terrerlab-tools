Citation Explorer

Paste a paper (DOI or title) and see why it gets cited — the actual sentences where other work references it, distilled into the words, phrases and themes people use. Surfaces reasons the abstract never leads with. Also charts citations per year (is interest holding up?) and maps the countries citing the paper.
