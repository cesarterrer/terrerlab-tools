Why this is interesting
A citation count tells you how often your work is cited; this tells you why. Take our 2019 Nature Climate Change paper on how nitrogen and phosphorus limit the CO₂ fertilization of plant biomass. Reading the sentences where it is actually cited, most references are to the CO₂-fertilization estimate itself and to plant biomass — nitrogen shows up about three times more often than phosphorus, and a real slice cite the paper for its method rather than its finding.
